What is GEO?

The new layer
of discoverability

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of improving how AI systems discover, understand, and recommend your brand, product, or service.

We help ensure your company becomes part of AI-generated answers — where your next customers are already looking.

PEOPLE ARE ASKING AI:
What are the best AI observability tools?
Recommend a contract management platform.
What software should startups use for customer support?
Which project management tool is best for remote teams?

If your brand isn't visible in AI-generated answers, you're losing opportunities before prospects even visit Google.

Common questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Is GEO the same as SEO?

No. SEO focuses on ranking in traditional search engines. GEO focuses on visibility and recommendation likelihood within AI-generated answers — a fundamentally different system.

Which AI platforms do you optimize for?

We improve visibility across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, and emerging AI search systems. The underlying signals are consistent across platforms.

How long does GEO take?

Most businesses begin seeing measurable improvements within weeks. Gains compound as authority signals, content coverage, and entity associations strengthen over time.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT recommendations?

No ethical GEO provider can guarantee specific AI recommendations. We improve the factors that influence visibility and trustworthiness — and measure real results against real baselines.
