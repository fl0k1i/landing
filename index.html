Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is GEO the same as SEO?

No. SEO focuses on ranking in traditional search. GEO focuses on visibility within AI-generated answers — a fundamentally different discovery system.

Which AI platforms do you optimize for?

We improve visibility across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, and emerging AI search systems.

How long does GEO take to show results?

Most businesses see measurable improvements within weeks. Gains compound as authority signals and content coverage strengthen over time.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT recommendations?

No ethical GEO provider can guarantee specific AI outputs. We improve the factors that influence visibility and measure real results against real baselines.
