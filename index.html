Why NewStream?

Traditional observability tools focus on prompts and analytics. NewStream captures the live execution stream of AI systems. Think of it as the event backbone for AI agents.

Send an Event

Just HTTP. No SDK required.

curl -X POST https://newstream-production.up.railway.app/sessions/demo/events \

-H "Content-Type: application/json" \
-H "x-api-key: YOUR_API_KEY" \

-d '{
  "type": "token",
  "text": "hello world",
  "model": "gpt-4"
}'
